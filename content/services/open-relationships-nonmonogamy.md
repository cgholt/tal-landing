Relationships don't have to follow one template.

Maybe you're curious about opening your relationship. Maybe you're already practicing ethical non-monogamy and have run into some unexpected challenges. Maybe you're navigating jealousy, mismatched desires, boundaries, agreements, or figuring out what you actually want your relationships to look like.

Or maybe you're simply questioning some of the assumptions you've carried about what relationships are supposed to be.

I work with individuals, couples, and other relationship structures, exploring open relationships, polyamory, kink, and other consensual, non-traditional relationship structures. We'll make room for honest conversations about desire, sex, jealousy, insecurity, autonomy, communication, boundaries, and the places where your ideals meet real life.

I'm not here to convince you that monogamy is the answer, or that non-monogamy is.

We'll start with what you want.

My goal is to create a space where you can explore your relationships and sexuality openly, without judgment or assumptions about what a "healthy" relationship has to look like.
